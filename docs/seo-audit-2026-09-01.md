## Findings

### 1. Blog titles truncate in search results — 14 of 18 posts

**Priority: highest.** Highest impact, lowest effort, and the only finding that affects a visitor's decision to click.

The `<SEO>` component appends `" | Christian Perez"` — **18 characters**, leaving a 42-character budget for the base title to stay under Google's ~60-character display limit. Most posts exceed it:

| Effective | Base | Post                                                       |
| --------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 79        | 61   | the-new-tokenomics-a-metric-that-will-define-enterprise-ai |
| 78        | 60   | dear-builder-welcome-to-quantum-computing                  |
| 78        | 60   | make-it-exist-then-make-it-perfect-building-with-partyrock |
| 75        | 57   | the-altivum-inc-tech-stack                                 |
| 74        | 56   | the-internets-new-users-are-not-humans                     |
| 74        | 56   | building-an-ai-chat-experience-for-your-personal-website   |
| 72        | 54   | the-modern-caste-system                                    |
| 71        | 53   | the-entrepreneurial-operating-system-in-the-age-of-ai      |
| 70        | 52   | the-paradox-of-good-days                                   |
| 68        | 50   | control-the-controllable-influence-the-variables           |
| 68        | 50   | from-keating-to-coding                                     |
| 65        | 47   | what-enterprise-sites-know-that-templates-don-t            |
| 64        | 46   | the-state-of-the-tech-quantum-computing-on-aws-2026        |
| 63        | 45   | the-incognito-button-should-be-more-prominent              |

Everything past ~60 characters is replaced with an ellipsis in the SERP.

**Fix — content only, no code change.** `BlogPost.tsx` already reads `post.seoTitle || post.title`. Setting a shorter `seoTitle` (≤ 42 chars) in Sanity fixes it per-post while the on-page `<h1>` keeps the full editorial title. Two posts (`building-an-ai-chat-experience…`, `make-it-exist-then-make-it-perfect…`) have no `seoTitle` at all and are falling back to the long title.

_Alternative worth considering:_ drop the brand suffix for `/blog/:slug` only. Article titles rarely benefit from it — the byline and `Article` schema already establish authorship — and it would reclaim all 18 characters at once, fixing most of the table in a one-line change. Downside: less brand reinforcement in the SERP.

**Fix — content only.** Expand `seoDescription` toward 150-160 characters with a value proposition or hook.

### 3. Core Web Vitals pass, but CLS and LCP have the least headroom

30 days of **real users**, bucketed by Google's own thresholds. Google requires ≥ 75% "good" at p75:

| Metric | good | needs-improvement | poor | % good  | Margin                        |
| ------ | ---- | ----------------- | ---- | ------- | ----------------------------- |
| TTFB   | 1316 | 48                | 31   | **94%** | comfortable                   |
| FCP    | 1221 | 51                | 37   | **93%** | comfortable                   |
| LCP    | 441  | 36                | 43   | **84%** | 9 pts                         |
| CLS    | 1030 | 162               | 41   | **83%** | 8 pts                         |
| INP    | 14   | 3                 | 0    | 82%     | _17 samples — not meaningful_ |

All five pass. CLS and LCP sit closest to the 75% floor, and CLS has 203 non-good samples over the period — which finding 4 partly explains.

### 4. Images missing explicit `width`/`height`

Contributes directly to the CLS margin above.

- **`src/components/Navigation.tsx:129`** — `<img src={logo} alt="TCG Logo" className="h-16 w-16" />` appears on **every page**. Tailwind sizes it in CSS, so the shift only occurs pre-stylesheet, but the attributes are free:
  ```jsx
  <img src={logo} alt="TCG Logo" className="h-16 w-16" width={64} height={64} />
  ```
- **Blog post body images** — 7 of 8 on a sampled post. These are Sanity CDN images; dimensions are available from the asset metadata and could be emitted by the Portable Text image component.
- **Podcast YouTube thumbnails** — lazy-loaded and below the fold, so lowest priority.

### 5. `/privacy` meta description is 97 characters

The only static route outside the target band. Minor — a policy page is not a traffic target.

---

## Recommended order

1. **Blog titles** (finding 1) — decide suffix-drop vs. per-post `seoTitle`, then apply. Biggest click-through impact.
2. **Blog descriptions** (finding 2) — start with the three under 80 characters.
3. **Logo `width`/`height`** (finding 4) — one line, helps the CLS margin.
4. **Blog image dimensions** (finding 4) — component change in the Portable Text renderer.
5. **`/privacy` description** (finding 5) — trivial.

Findings 1, 2, and 5 are content edits in Sanity and need editorial judgment on wording. Findings 3 and 4 are code.
